Learning Path

Your 5-Step Charter Journey

Each module covers one charter component. Work through them in order — each AI agent builds on the previous.

01
Getting Started ⏱ ~10 min
Login, Dashboard & Your First Charter
Understand the CharterOS workspace — how to log in, rename your charter, read the dashboard and navigate between components.
+
1
Login with your team password
Your TPO administrator provides a unique team password. Enter it on the login screen and click Sign In →. Each department has its own password.
2
Rename your charter
On the dashboard, click ✏ Rename next to "Untitled Charter". Give it a meaningful name — this appears on all exported documents.
3
Understand the 5 component cards
The dashboard shows 5 cards: Team & TOR, Problem Statement, Vision & Strategy, Value Realisation, and Roadmap. Work through them in order — each must reach 100% before the next AI agent can use it as context.
4
Try Load Sample Data
Click Load Sample to see a fully populated example charter. This is the fastest way to understand what each completed section should look like before you start your own.
Key Actions
Sign In → ✏ Rename Load Sample Export Backup ✓ Auto-saves every 3 seconds ✓ Progress resets only via Clear Charter
02
Step 1 of 5 ⏱ ~15 min
Team Structure & Terms of Reference
Add your team members, set meeting cadence, and let AI generate your complete Terms of Reference — covering mandate, roles, decision authority and escalation paths.
+
1
Add all team members
Click + Add Member. For each person enter their name, role and key responsibilities. Add every person involved — the AI uses the full team list to generate accountability structures.
2
Set meeting cadence
Click + Add Meeting. Define your standups, sprint reviews and planning sessions — enter the meeting name, frequency and purpose for each.
3
Generate TOR
Click Generate TOR. The AI drafts a complete 5-section Terms of Reference in seconds. The 5 sections are: Purpose & Mandate, Roles & Responsibilities, Ways of Working, Decision Making Authority, and Reporting & Escalation.
4
Review and Approve TOR
Read through all 5 sections carefully. If needed, click Regenerate to get a new draft. When satisfied, click Approve TOR — this locks the TOR, completes Team & TOR at 100%, and unlocks Step 2.
Key Actions
+ Add Member + Add Meeting Generate TOR Approve TOR
name · role · responsibilities meeting name · frequency · purpose ✓ TOR is locked on approval
03
Step 2 of 5 ⏱ ~25 min
Problem Statement
Upload your source materials — meeting notes, research files, interview transcripts, strategy docs — and let AI synthesise a structured Problem Statement with user pain points and business gap.
+
1
Upload source materials
Click Upload Source Materials. Supports TXT, DOC, DOCX, XLSX, PDF. Upload everything relevant — meeting notes, stakeholder interviews, research findings. You can upload multiple files at once and the AI reads across all of them.
2
Paste raw notes (optional)
Tick Include raw notes and paste any unstructured notes directly into the text box — no formatting needed. This is useful for quick observations or WhatsApp notes you haven't formatted into a doc yet.
3
Generate Draft
Click Generate Draft. The AI synthesises your uploaded materials into a 5-section Problem Statement: Executive Summary, User Pain Points, Business Gap, Impact of Inaction, and Key Assumptions.
4
Refine and Approve
Switch to the Refine & Approve tab. Read the full draft. Edit any section directly if needed, or click Regenerate for a new draft. When satisfied, click Approve & Save — this locks the Problem Statement and feeds it into the Vision agent.
Key Actions
Upload Source Materials Include raw notes Generate Draft Refine & Approve Approve & Save
TXT · DOC · DOCX · XLSX · PDF ✓ More files = better output ✓ Feeds into Vision agent on approval
04
Steps 3 & 4 of 5 ⏱ ~40 min
Vision & Strategy + Value Realisation
Craft your long-term vision with strategic pillars, then define KPIs and generate a full Cost Benefit Analysis from your uploaded Excel estimates.
+

VISION & STRATEGY

1
Enter product details
Fill in Product Name, Industry/Domain and Target Users at the top of the Vision section.
2
Upload strategy documents
Upload executive briefings, strategic papers, and workshop notes. The AI reads these alongside your approved Problem Statement — so the vision is always grounded in the problem.
3
Generate Draft, then Approve & Save
Click Generate Draft. Review on the Refine & Approve tab. Click Approve & Save to lock the Vision and feed it into the KPI Recommender agent.

VALUE REALISATION

4
Generate KPI Recommendations
On the AI Recommend tab, click Generate Recommendations. The KPI agent reads both your approved Problem Statement and Vision and recommends 6-8 SMART KPIs with targets, timeframes and owners.
5
Review KPI Table and upload CBA data
Switch to KPI Table to review each KPI. Then go to Cost Benefit Analysis → enter benefit values per KPI and upload your cost estimates Excel file.
6
Generate CBA and Approve
Click Generate CBA. Review the financial narrative, bar charts, donut chart and 3-year net position. Click Approve CBA to complete Value Realisation at 100%.
Key Actions
Generate Draft Approve & Save Generate Recommendations KPI Table Generate CBA Approve CBA
Product Name · Industry · Target Users benefit values · cost estimates Excel ✓ KPI agent reads Problem + Vision
05
Step 5 of 5 ⏱ ~30 min
Product Roadmap & Export
Generate a phased product roadmap with a visual Gantt chart, complete two approval gates, then export your finished charter as PDF, slides or Excel.
+
1
Set roadmap parameters
Enter start date, total duration, number of phases and team size. The Roadmap Architect agent uses team size to calibrate task durations and parallel workstreams.
2
Enter your epics and features
Type your key epics, features and deliverables in the Epics / Features text box. One item per line works best. Be specific — the AI uses these directly to structure phases and assign tasks.
3
Generate Roadmap
Click Generate Roadmap. The AI structures phases, assigns tasks with durations, and identifies milestones. Review the phases and task list on the Phases view.
4
Gate 1 — Approve Phases & Tasks
Review all phases and tasks. Click Approve Phases & Tasks. This is Gate 1 of 2. The Gantt chart is then auto-generated from the approved tasks.
5
Gate 2 — Approve Timeline
Switch to the Gantt View. Review the week-by-week timeline and phase bands. Click Approve Timeline — this is Gate 2 of 2 and completes the Roadmap at 100%.
6
Export your charter
Navigate to Export Charter in the sidebar. Choose your format: Export PDF (full charter with Gantt and CBA charts), Generate Slides (10-slide management deck), or Export Backup (JSON restore point).
Key Actions
Generate Roadmap Approve Phases & Tasks Gantt View Approve Timeline Export PDF Generate Slides
start date · duration · phases · team size epics / features ✓ Two approval gates required ✓ Export only available at 100%

FAQ

Common Questions

Why do I have to complete sections in order? +
Each AI agent reads the approved output of the previous section as context. The Vision Strategist agent reads your approved Problem Statement. The KPI Recommender reads both Problem and Vision. This context chain is what makes the outputs coherent — your KPIs will always be traceable back to the problem you defined.
What files can I upload? +
CharterOS supports TXT, DOC, DOCX, XLSX, CSV and PDF files. You can also paste raw notes directly into the text box on any input section — no formatting required. Upload as much as you have — the more source material you provide, the richer the AI output.
What happens if I'm not happy with the AI draft? +
Click Regenerate as many times as you need before approving. Each regeneration produces a fresh draft using the same inputs. You can also edit the draft directly in the Refine & Approve tab. Nothing is locked until you explicitly click Approve.
Can I go back and edit an approved section? +
Yes — click the section name in the left sidebar to return to it at any time. You can regenerate or edit the content. Note that if you change an approved section that a later section has already used as context, consider regenerating the later sections too to keep the charter coherent.
Will I lose my work if I close the browser? +
No. CharterOS auto-saves to the cloud every few seconds. You can safely close the browser, switch devices, or take a break — your charter will be exactly as you left it when you log back in. We also recommend using Export Backup periodically to save a local JSON snapshot.
What does the TPO Admin Portal show? +
TPO administrators can see a live dashboard of all departments and charters — showing section-by-section completion % for every team. Admins can drill down into any department tile and open any charter in read-only view without a team password. The dashboard auto-refreshes every 60 seconds.
How do I export my finished charter? +
Once your charter reaches 100% completion, navigate to Export Charter in the sidebar. You can export as a PDF (full charter document with Gantt chart and CBA charts), a PowerPoint deck (10-slide management presentation), download the CBA Excel model, or save a JSON backup to restore or share your charter state.
What are the 6 AI agents and what do they do? +
CharterOS uses 6 sequential AI agents:

01 TOR Drafter — generates the 5-section Terms of Reference from team members and meetings.
02 Problem Analyst — synthesises pain points and business gap from your uploaded files.
03 Vision Strategist — reads the approved Problem and crafts vision, mission and strategic pillars.
04 KPI Recommender — reads Problem + Vision and recommends 6-8 SMART KPIs.
05 CBA Analyst — parses benefit values and cost data to generate the financial narrative and charts.
06 Roadmap Architect — structures phases, tasks, durations and milestones from your epics.
