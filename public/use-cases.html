Scenarios. Ready to Try.

Download the sample input files for any team, upload them into CharterOS, and watch the AI build a complete Product Charter from real research notes.

How it works: Each team has a set of raw notes, research files and Excel templates — the same kind of documents a real Product Office team would have. Upload them into CharterOS and generate the full charter. No preparation needed.
Getting Started

How to Use These Files

Three steps from download to AI-generated charter.

📥
1. Download a team's input pack
Each ZIP contains 6 folders — one per charter section — with TXT notes, KPI and CBA Excel files, and PowerPoint inputs for Vision and Roadmap. Pick the use case most relevant to you.
🚀
2. Login to CharterOS with the team password
Use the password shown on each use case card to log in. You'll land on that team's charter workspace, ready to go.
🤖
3. Upload the files and generate each section
On each charter section, upload the relevant files from the ZIP. Click Generate — watch the AI synthesise your notes into a structured charter.

A
Digital Services
Team A · Digital Services Office
HDB Maintenance & Estate Services
Digital Transformation
Singapore's HDB estate maintenance operates through 5 disconnected channels — phone, email, myHDBPage, WhatsApp and walk-ins. Officers spend 2-3 hours daily on manual triage. Residents have no visibility. This charter defines a unified AI-powered platform to transform the experience for residents, TC officers and contractors.
Team Password teamA2026
Core Problem
  • 3.2-day average acknowledgement time for maintenance requests
  • 38% duplicate report rate across 5 channels
  • Resident satisfaction score 2.9/5
  • TC officers spend 2-3 hours/day on manual triage
  • Zero visibility for contractors on job status
Key KPIs to Generate
  • Duplicate request reduction rate
  • Request acknowledgement time (3+ days → 6 hours)
  • Officer manual triage time saved
  • Resident satisfaction score improvement
  • Platform adoption rate by residents
