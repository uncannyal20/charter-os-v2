Vision: To make lift breakdowns in HDB estates a thing of the past — through an intelligent maintenance system that sees failure coming, acts before it happens, and keeps every resident moving safely.

Pillars:
1. Real-time lift health telemetry at scale
2. AI-powered predictive failure detection
3. Proactive contractor dispatch and SLA management
4. Resident safety and communication

North Star: Reduce lift breakdown rate from 60 to 35 per 1,000 lifts within 24 months of platform go-live.
